
TOUCHDESIGNER OPERATOR TYPES - YOU HAVE THE WRONG ONE!

CURRENT: OSC In DAT (green, data operator)
NEEDED:  OSC In CHOP (blue, channel operator)

WHY THIS MATTERS:
- DATs are for text/data manipulation (green)
- CHOPs are for audio/video signals (blue) 
- COMPs need CHOP inputs, not DAT inputs

CORRECT SETUP:
1. Delete the green OSC In DAT
2. Create blue OSC In CHOP instead
3. Connect CHOP output -> COMP input

The blue CHOP will have the circle on the RIGHT side!
